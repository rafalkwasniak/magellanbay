When Rafał says "look at how it's done in kociaczek" (or any other product/portal), he means it as a SUGGESTION/inspiration, not a binding requirement. Each production is different and everything must be adapted to THIS product (MyShop / shop.kwasniak.org).

**Why:** he reuses proven patterns across his products but the fit is never 1:1; blindly copying would import decisions that do not match this product's needs.

**How to apply:** study the referenced pattern, extract what genuinely fits MyShop, and adapt (or drop) the rest. Always reason about this product's spec first; present the adaptation, not a verbatim copy. (Example already applied: the email outbox pattern [[email-outbox-cron-pattern]] was taken but extended for our communication-history and multi-tenant needs.)
